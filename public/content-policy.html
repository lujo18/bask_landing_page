Content Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Content Policy explains the nature of the content you may encounter in the Bask mobile application, how we create it, and how you should use it.

Bask is developed by Bask ("we," "us," or "our"). The app is bundle ID com.bask.migraine, and our website is baskmigraine.app. For questions about this policy, contact support@baskmigraine.app.

1. Purpose

Bask provides educational content designed to help users better understand migraine, headache disorders, related symptoms, and factors that may influence migraine patterns. This content may include written articles, brief lessons, explanations of migraine science, summaries of clinical guidelines, overviews of trigger-management strategies, and descriptive risk indicators.

The content is intended to support self-awareness and informed conversations with healthcare providers. It is not a diagnostic or treatment service.

2. Educational Nature

All content provided through Bask is for general educational and informational purposes only. It is not tailored to any individual's specific medical condition, health history, or treatment needs. The information we present is designed to be accessible, accurate at the time of publication, and grounded in commonly accepted migraine science.

We do our best to explain concepts clearly, but educational content cannot cover every individual situation or replace personalized professional guidance.

3. Sourcing and Accuracy

Where applicable, Bask content may reference established clinical resources, guidelines, and classifications, including but not limited to the American Headache Society (AHS), the International Classification of Headache Disorders, 3rd edition (ICHD-3), and peer-reviewed medical literature.

We make reasonable efforts to ensure that the content we create is accurate and up to date when published. However, medical knowledge evolves over time. Bask does not guarantee that any article, summary, indicator, or explanation is complete, error-free, or reflective of the most recent clinical guidance. Content is provided without any warranty of accuracy, reliability, or fitness for a particular purpose.

4. No Medical Advice

Bask does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Content in the app is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare provider.

You should never rely on Bask content to diagnose a health condition, start or stop a medication, change a treatment plan, or make any medical decision. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional for personal medical advice.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or seek immediate medical attention.

5. No User-Generated Content

Bask does not host user-generated public content. All educational content, articles, lessons, risk indicator explanations, and similar materials are created by Bask. Any health data, logs, notes, or other information you enter into the app is stored locally on your device and is not shared with other users or the public.

Because Bask does not operate public forums, comment sections, or social feeds, we do not have user-generated content to moderate in the traditional sense. You remain responsible for ensuring that any information you enter into your private app use is lawful and does not infringe the rights of others.
